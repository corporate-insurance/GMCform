Why medical expenses matter

Medical costs tend to rise over time, and one unplanned hospital stay can use up years of savings. A single admission can bring many different bills:

🛏️ Room & ICU🔪 Surgery & doctor fees💊 Medicines🧪 Tests & scans🚑 Ambulance📋 Follow-up care
Health is the real wealth. Protect it before you need it.
Insurance does not stop illness, but it keeps worry about the bills away.
